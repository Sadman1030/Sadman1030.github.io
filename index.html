Published work

One peer reviewed paper, in a Q1 journal.

A graph clustering neural network framework for mapping urban functional zones in a fast-evolving urban landscape of Bangladesh

Mafrid Haydar, Sakib Hosan, Al Hossain Rafi, Md. Kamran Hasan Khan and Sadman Mubarrat Mahee. City and Environment Interactions 30, 100401. May 2026. Open access, CC BY 4.0.

Read the paper (DOI 10.1016/j.cacint.2026.100401)

The question

Khulna is growing fast and unevenly. Planners need to know which parts of the city do what, but standard clustering on land use alone ignores how places connect. We asked whether a model that reads the road network as a graph could find functional zones more reliably.

The method

Road intersections and points of interest become nodes, and road links become edges. Each node carries values from nighttime lights, land cover and elevation. A two layer graph convolutional network, built in PyTorch Geometric, learns an embedding for every node. K-means on those embeddings gives five zones, with the number of clusters chosen by the elbow method and silhouette score.

The check

The land cover input was validated on a stratified sample of reference points. The zones were then compared with Maxar satellite imagery, the MAP-WORLD basemap and the Detailed Area Plan of the Khulna Development Authority.

What it found

The residential zone and the commercial and residential zone are the most built up, at 49.92 and 45.52 percent. Residential areas largely lack essential services, health facilities most of all, while the business and service zone has them. The zone map above is the model output.

Thesis: forecasting rain on the delta

Undergraduate thesis, KUET. Supervisor: Mafrid Haydar.

A Phase-Separated Spatiotemporal Graph-Transformer Ensemble for Daily to Monthly Rainfall Forecasting in Coastal Bangladesh

Free reanalysis rainfall is the usual input for flood and water planning in coastal Bangladesh, but it is badly biased there and gauges are sparse. Against five coastal gauges, raw ERA5 daily rainfall scores a coefficient of determination of minus 0.383, worse than predicting the average. This thesis builds a forecasting framework that turns that unusable field into a calibrated forecast with honest error bars, using 21 years of data from 1998 to 2018.

Where it fails

Skill dropped in the 2018 test year. The diagnosis in the thesis is that the surface pressure input shifted away from its training distribution, confirmed with a Kolmogorov-Smirnov test, and that 2018 had far fewer extreme days. Days above 75 millimetres fell from 71 in 2017 to 8 in 2018, which includes the period around Cyclone Titli.

Other limits are stated in the thesis: five gauges in one division, one reanalysis product, one day ahead forecasts, and gauge gaps assumed to be random. I treat the 2018 result as a signal that a deployed version needs input monitoring.

Khulna Development Authority, intern

22 February to 14 March 2026, Khulna

I worked on the design and feasibility check of a CNG and auto-rickshaw stand at Dakbangla Mor, a busy intersection in the central commercial zone where vehicles queue on the roadside with no designated stand.

  • Digitized road elements and built the GIS database for the road infrastructure.
  • Sketched and mapped road elements to support engineering design and planning documentation.
  • Prepared longitudinal profile and cross section drawings in Autodesk Civil 3D to support road alignment design.
  • Used GIS tools to organize, manage and analyze spatial data through the design and planning phase.

The team report, submitted on 8 March 2026, rests on surveys of traffic volume, vehicle queues and parking, passenger demand and pedestrian movement. It proposes a layout with separate entry and exit points, boarding areas and pedestrian paths.
